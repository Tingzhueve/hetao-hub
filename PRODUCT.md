# HeTao Hub (核桃情报局)

## Vision

HeTao Hub/ Walnut Intelligence is a mobile-first fan intelligence hub for Zhang Linghe (张凌赫).

The goal is not to replace Weibo, Xiaohongshu, or Douyin.

The goal is to help fans quickly answer:

- Did Zhang Linghe post anything today?
- Did the studio release new photos?
- Is there a live stream tonight?
- Has a new episode been released?
- What activities are coming up?
- What happened recently that I might have missed?

Walnut Intelligence focuses on high-value official content and event tracking in one place.

---

# Target Users

Primary users:

- Zhang Linghe fans
- Casual followers
- Fans who do not want to constantly check multiple platforms

---

# Core Principles

## What We Aggregate

Official and high-signal content only:

- Zhang Linghe
- Zhang Linghe Studio
- Brand official accounts
- Platform official accounts
- Program official accounts

Examples:

- Weibo
- Xiaohongshu
- Douyin
- Instagram
- Tencent Video
- Taobao Live

---

## What We Do NOT Aggregate

The product is not intended to be a social network.

Excluded content:

- Fan posts
- Super Topic posts
- Comments
- Discussions
- Community threads

Reason:

High-quality signal is more valuable than high-volume noise.

# App Structure

## Feed

Purpose:

Real-time official updates.

Examples:

- Studio photos
- Brand campaign releases
- Live stream announcements
- New episode releases
- Instagram updates

Features:

- Platform filters
- Favorite posts
- Image preview
- Related activity links

Future:

- Multi-image gallery
- Notification integration

---

## Calendar

Purpose:

Activity timeline and archive.

Calendar contains two sections:

### Upcoming

Known future activities.

Examples:

- Brand live stream
- Variety show release
- Magazine release
- Offline event

Status:

- Confirmed
- Pending announcement

Actions:

- Add to Calendar
- Set Reminder

---

### Past

Historical activity archive.

Examples:

- Completed live stream
- Released episode
- Brand event
- Magazine launch

Actions:

- View Details
- Related Updates

---

## Favorites

Purpose:

Personal collection of saved content.

Examples:

- Favorite photos
- Favorite activities
- Important announcements
